# Business Insights

## Q1 Sales Performance

- **Q1 2022 Sales:** $330,500
- **Q1 2023 Sales:** $453,830
- **Year-over-Year Growth:** 37.32%

### Key Observation

Q1 sales increased from **$330,500 in 2022 to $453,830 in 2023**, representing a **37.32% year-over-year increase**.

This indicates a strong improvement in overall Q1 sales performance compared with the previous year.

---

## Monthly Performance

| Month | 2022 Sales | 2023 Sales | YoY Growth |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| January | $101,595 | $143,555 | 41.30% |
| February | $113,445 | $145,535 | 28.29% |
| March | $115,460 | $164,740 | 42.68% |

### Key Observation

**March recorded the highest year-over-year growth at 42.68%**, followed by January at 41.30%.

February also showed positive growth of 28.29%, although it had the lowest year-over-year growth among the three months.

March also recorded the **highest monthly sales in both years**, increasing from **$115,460 in 2022 to $164,740 in 2023**.

---

## Business Perspective

The analysis provides management with a quick view of:

1. Overall Q1 sales growth
2. Monthly sales performance
3. Year-over-year changes
4. Months showing stronger or weaker growth

The results indicate that **all three months experienced positive year-over-year growth**, with March showing the strongest growth rate.

These findings can be used as a starting point for investigating the factors behind monthly sales changes and supporting future sales planning.
